# Theme → ticker map

When a post is Tier 2/3 (a policy or theme rather than a single named company),
use this to translate the topic into the names that typically react. These are
*candidate reaction baskets*, not recommendations — always confirm the current
setup and check `track-record.md` for how durable that theme's reactions are.

> Direction assumes a *favorable* Trump post. If he **attacks** the theme/company
> instead, flip the sign — and remember attacks tend to dump harder than praise
> pumps.

## Crypto / digital assets
- **Trigger words:** bitcoin, crypto, strategic reserve, "Anti-Crypto Army",
  stablecoin, CFTC, prediction markets, World Liberty, $TRUMP.
- **Reacts up:** COIN, MSTR, HOOD; miners MARA/RIOT/CIFR/CLSK; his own DJT
  (holds BTC). Coins: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP.
- **Durability:** ⚠️ low — usually a one-day spike that fades. Fast reaction trade.

## AI infrastructure / data centers
- **Trigger words:** Stargate, AI infrastructure, "largest in history", data
  centers, chips, compute, electricity for AI.
- **Reacts up:** ORCL, NVDA, AVGO, PLTR, DELL, SMCI; power/grid CEG, VST, GEV,
  NRG; he disclosed owning ORCL, NVDA, AVGO, PLTR.
- **Durability:** medium — theme persists past the headline.

## Tariffs / trade
- **Exemption / pause / relief (UP):** the relieved sector rallies. Electronics →
  AAPL, DELL, HPQ; autos → GM, F, TSLA; semis → NVDA, AVGO, broad chips.
- **New / higher tariffs (DOWN):** importers, retailers, autos, China-exposed
  names fall; domestic substitutes may rise.
- **Durability:** ⚠️ fragile — frequently walked back within days.

## Energy / power
- **Trigger words:** "drill baby drill", energy dominance, LNG, nuclear, grid.
- **Reacts up:** XOM, CVX, OXY, COP; LNG, ET, KMI; nuclear/uranium CEG, VST,
  CCJ, OKLO, SMR; coal occasionally.

## Defense / space / security
- **Trigger words:** peace through strength, Golden Dome, military buildup,
  drones, border.
- **Reacts up:** LMT, RTX, NOC, GD, LHX; PLTR, AXON; space RKLB, LUNR.
- **De-escalation / "deal done":** broad risk-on; oil can fall (supply fear off).

## Reshoring / "Made in America" / manufacturing
- **Reacts up:** US steel (NUE, STLD, CLF, X), domestic semis (INTC, GFS, TXN),
  caterpillar-type industrials (CAT, DE), shipbuilders.

## Pharma / healthcare
- **Drug-price controls / "most favored nation" (DOWN):** large-cap pharma
  (PFE, MRK, LLY, JNJ) tends to fall.
- **Deregulation / FDA-friendly (UP):** biotech/XBI risk-on.

## Media / "his" companies & conflict-of-interest overlaps
- **DJT (Trump Media):** spikes on any DJT-favorable post; ⚠️ structurally weak —
  trade the spike, do not hold.
- **Disclosed media buys (Paramount, WBD, NFLX, DIS, CMCSA):** sensitive to
  Trump praise/attacks and to FCC/merger policy he influences.

## Financials
- **Deregulation / rate-cut pressure (UP):** banks BAC, GS, JPM (he disclosed
  BAC, GS); small-caps IWM; rate-cut talk → gold (GLD, GDX), long-duration growth.
